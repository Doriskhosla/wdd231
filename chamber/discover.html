Discover Sandy

Local History

Sandy began as a mining and railroad town in the late 1800s. After mining declined, it grew into a farming community and later a modern suburban city. Today, Sandy blends its historic roots with strong neighborhoods, businesses, and mountain access.

Demographics

Sandy has a population of about 96,000 residents, making it one of the largest and fastest‑growing communities in the Salt Lake Valley.
